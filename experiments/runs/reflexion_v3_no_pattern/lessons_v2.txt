1. Keep any single position at or below about 70% of the portfolio. When one goes above about 65%, trim part of the winner back toward 55–60%, and keep the proceeds invested in the other holdings.
2. Hold a diversified core and avoid same-week switches between holdings. Rotate a position only after it has lagged for several weeks, not because of one weak or strong week.
3. When a holding is falling for several weeks in a row, sell it in steps instead of waiting. Don't rebuy a holding that is still declining.
4. Once ahead of buy-and-hold, prefer making no trades in weeks without a clear sustained signal. Most of the lead was built while the agent held its portfolio.
