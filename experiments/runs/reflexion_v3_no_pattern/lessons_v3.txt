1. When a holding falls for several weeks in a row, sell it in steps and don't rebuy it while it is still declining. Put the proceeds into holdings that are holding up, or keep them in cash.
2. When you are well ahead of buy-and-hold and there is no clear multi-week signal, make no trades. Most of the lead was built while holding.
3. Keep the largest position under about 50%. Don't build concentration by buying a stock after one strong week or by selling one holding and buying another in the same week. A single-week jump should not push any stock toward 75%.
