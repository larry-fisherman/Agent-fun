<lessons>
1. Don't sell a stock right after a sharp one-week drop and move the money into the stock that just jumped. Large one-week moves often reverse the next week, so wait for confirmation or hold.
2. Avoid trading every week. Constant rotation between stocks lost steadily to a static equal-weight hold. Change positions only for a clear, sustained reason, and otherwise keep holdings near equal weight.
3. Don't sell into a down week and then hold cash or an underweight position. Rebounds are often sharp and broad, and staying under-invested misses them, so reinvest the proceeds or keep exposure close to full.
