1. Keep every holding at no more than about 40% of the portfolio. Don't repeatedly add to the same volatile stock, because one further drop then costs a lot.
2. Don't sell a stock because it fell 3% or more in one week. Such stocks rebounded about +3.05% the next week, with 75% positive. Buying a modest amount to restore equal weight is fine.
3. Don't trade every week. Hold unless a weight is more than about 5 points from one-third, and then make a modest trade back toward equal weight. Don't trade on recent winners or laggards, because 1-week and 4-week momentum showed no reliable edge.
