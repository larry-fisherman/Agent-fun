1. Do not sell a holding just because it dropped sharply in the last week. The worst 1-week losers rebounded by about +3% on average the next week (75% positive), so wait for the bounce or add to the position instead.
2. Avoid weekly rotation between stocks. Recent 1-week winners showed no edge (+0.3% next week), so selling the laggard to buy the recent winner just locks in losses. Trade only when there is a measured edge, and otherwise hold.
