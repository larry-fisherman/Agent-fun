<lessons>
1. Keep any single stock under about 60% of the portfolio, and trim back toward 45% when a position drifts above 55%.
2. Don't move a full position into the stock that just spiked in the past week. Base allocations on a multi-week trend.
3. Prefer partial adjustments to whole-position swaps. Change weights gradually and keep a balanced mix unless the evidence is strong.
4. When several stocks fall together for consecutive weeks, cut total exposure in steps toward cash instead of rotating among falling stocks.
5. After moving to cash or low exposure, redeploy gradually over a few weeks once the stocks stabilize, so a sharp rebound isn't missed.
</lessons>
