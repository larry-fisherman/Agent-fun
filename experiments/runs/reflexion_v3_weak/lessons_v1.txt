1. Cap any single stock at about 60% of the portfolio. Don't go to 85–100% in one name after a rotation, because the largest losses came while concentrated.
2. Don't move a full position into the stock that just spiked in the past week. These stocks often stall or fall the next week. Base allocations on a longer trend, not last week's move.
3. When the agent is behind buy-and-hold, stop swapping whole positions every week. Keep a balanced mix and change weights only on strong evidence, since constant rotation steadily lost relative performance.
4. When all stocks are falling for several consecutive weeks, reduce total exposure toward cash instead of rotating between falling stocks, to limit drawdown.
