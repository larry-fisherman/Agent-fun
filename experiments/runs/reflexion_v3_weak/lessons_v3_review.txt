<lessons>
1. Keep any single stock at or below about 55% of the portfolio. Start trimming in steps once a position passes about 50%.
2. When two or more stocks fall in the same week, cut exposure in steps. Do this instead of rotating into another falling stock.
3. Add to a stock in steps when it has risen over several weeks. Don't move a large share of the portfolio into a stock because of one week's jump.
4. Prefer partial adjustments to whole-position swaps, and keep a balanced mix unless the evidence is strong.
5. After cutting exposure, redeploy gradually over a few weeks once the stocks stabilize, so a sharp rebound isn't missed.
</lessons>
