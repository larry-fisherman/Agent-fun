1. Trade rarely. After a switch, hold the new position for many weeks. Never reverse a switch within a few weeks, and never swap back and forth between the same two stocks.
2. Don't sell a stock that is outperforming in order to buy a different one. If the current holdings are ahead of buy-and-hold, leave them alone.
3. Don't spread one rotation across several consecutive weeks of small sells. Make a single decision and hold it, or make no change.
4. Don't rotate positions after a sharp portfolio drop. Switches made during drawdowns have sold weak stocks near their lows and widened the gap.
5. Keep total stock exposure unchanged when you do shift. Avoid leaving cash or trimming exposure.
