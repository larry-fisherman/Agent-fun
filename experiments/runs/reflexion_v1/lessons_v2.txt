1. Trade rarely. When you do shift, move money from one stock to another in similar dollar amounts so total stock exposure stays the same, then hold the new mix for many weeks without further tinkering.
2. Don't make small partial sells that leave cash or lower stock exposure unless there is a specific reason. Trims like this lagged buy-and-hold with no benefit.
3. Keep holding a concentrated allocation that is outperforming. Don't trim winners to rebalance toward equal weights.
